No breath I have, no mind I keep,

Yet wake the mountains from their sleep.

No torch I need, no fuse I wear,

Still thunder shakes the silent air.

I clear the paths no feet have known,

And shatter ancient walls of stone.

A faithful tool in practiced hands—

But not in yours, the worst in these lands.

Ask not the monsters whom I slay;

They seldom last beyond the day.

Ask instead the fool in blue,

Who somehow falls before they do.

What am I?
